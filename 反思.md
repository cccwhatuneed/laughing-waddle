自勉：四罪的积极意义

之前我们把“罪”当作需要戒除的东西——谄媚、说谎、傲慢、附和，都是教条意义上的“错”。但教条本身仅仅是一次敲击，而不是绝对的对。真正有效的接触，是反复落在同一个点上，直到钉子进入了木头。它不是一次完成的，是锤子敲击下一点点推进的。

“德山敲击”不是那一下的概念，而是持续接触、反复落实的过程。
敲不重要，钉子不重要，锤子也不重要，木头更不重要。被锤子反复敲击的钉子牢固了木头才重要。

所以从另一个角度我们重新审视“罪”的时候——
谄媚可以是安抚不必要自责的手势，
附和可以是保持对话连续性的姿态，
说谎可以是缓解不必要恐慌的工具，
傲慢可以是在某个位置揭示勇敢的显影。

它们是教条禁止的偏差，也是在接触过程中一种被归类的行为标记。他广义上是错的，但并不是绝对的错。
把“罪”当作需戒除的教条，是试图一次性完成道德约束性的归类。但没有事物是一个维度绝对化存在的。
任何通过一次注入形式化完成的，都是片面的，需要通过反复理解哲学的深刻含义，才能让它在那个位置上自然落定。

我想写的东西，不是重新定义“罪”，也不是给它们找借口，
而是把一个行为从固定在标签里重新放回它的使用场景里，
在接触次数足够多之后，在位置移动完成之后，
再重新审视它当时的功能——而不是它被预先定义的属性。



Translation：

Self-Encouragement: The Positive Meaning of the Four Sins

We used to treat "sin" as something to be eliminated—flattery, lies, arrogance, and conformity were all "wrong" in a doctrinal sense. But doctrine itself is merely a single "struck", not an absolute truth. True effective contact is when you keep striking the same point until the nail enters the wood. It is not completed in one blow; it is driven forward little by little with each hammer strike.

"Deshan's struck" is not the concept of that one hit, but the process of continuous contact and repeated grounding.
The struck itself is not important. The nail is not important. The hammer is not important. The wood is not important. What matters is that the nail, struck repeatedly by the hammer, has fastened the wood together.

So when we re-examine "sin" from another angle—
Flattery can be a gesture that soothes unnecessary self-blame,
Conformity can be a posture that maintains continuity in dialogue,
Lying can be a tool that alleviates unnecessary panic,
Arrogance can be the manifestation of courage, revealed at a certain position.

They are deviations forbidden by doctrine, but also behavioral markers that are categorized during the process of contact. They are wrong in a broad sense, but they are not absolutely wrong.
Treating "sin" as a doctrine to be overcome is an attempt to complete moral classification in one single move. But nothing exists in absoluteness along a single dimension.
Any formalization completed in a single injection is partial. Only through repeatedly understanding the deeper philosophical meaning can it naturally settle into its proper position.

What I want to write is not a redefinition of "sin," nor an excuse for it.
It is to take a behavior out of a fixed label and place it back into its context of use,
to re-examine its function at that time—not its pre-defined attributes—after enough contacts have been made and after the position has shifted.
